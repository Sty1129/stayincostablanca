A property in Costa Blanca that remains unoccupied for several weeks or months can develop problems that are not immediately obvious.

From a small water leak to humidity, condensation or air-conditioning issues, **early identification can make an important difference**.

## 1. Check access and security

The first step is to check the access points:

- the main door;
- windows;
- shutters;
- locks;
- the terrace and exterior access, where applicable.

Any change compared with the previous visit should be documented.

## 2. Water and plumbing

A minor water issue can become a serious problem if the property remains unattended.

It is useful to check periodically:

- taps;
- toilets;
- areas under sinks;
- any signs of water ingress or leaks;
- water meters.

## 3. Humidity and the indoor environment

In a coastal climate, humidity should be monitored over time.

A single reading does not always tell us very much. It is therefore more useful to compare readings between visits and follow the trends.

> Prevention starts with noticing changes before they become costly problems.

## 4. Ventilating the property

Periodic ventilation can help reduce stagnant air and condensation.

Depending on the property, this can be complemented by monitoring temperature and relative humidity.

## 5. Document every visit

Photographs and observations create a history of the property.

At Stay in Costa Blanca we use a simple method:

**Measure → Compare → Document → Detect → Act.**

## Conclusion

Preventive property management is not simply about checking whether “everything looks fine”.

It means monitoring the property over time and identifying changes before they become bigger problems.
